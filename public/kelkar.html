Prof. Yashwanth Rao Kelkar

The Architect of ABVP

"Students are the future architects of the nation."

Prof. Yashwantrao Kelkar (1920–1987) was an Indian academic, social activist, and the chief ideological architect of the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP), one of India's largest student organizations. He transformed the body into a massive, well-knit student movement grounded in nationalist values and youth empowerment.
